Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before booking your ride

Fill in the booking form on our booking page and it will open WhatsApp with your trip details ready to send, along with a booking reference. You can also call us on +94 72 478 6262 or message +94 76 078 1959 directly. We reply to every booking request within one hour.

We are based in Hiriketiya, Dikwella and serve the whole island. Our most requested routes are Colombo Airport transfers, the southern coast (Mirissa, Unawatuna, Galle Fort), Yala Safari, Ella and the hill country, and the cultural triangle around Sigiriya and Dambulla.

Our Toyota Prius seats up to 3 passengers and our Toyota KDH van seats up to 7. For groups of 8 or more we arrange additional vehicles — select "Any Available" on the booking form and we will confirm the best setup for your group.

Yes. Bandaranaike International Airport (CMB) transfers are one of our most popular services and we operate them 24 hours a day. The drive between the airport and Dikwella takes roughly 4 hours. Share your flight number when booking so we can adjust the pickup time if your flight is delayed.

Fares depend on distance, vehicle and how long you need us. The booking form gives you an instant estimate as you fill it in, and you are welcome to message us on WhatsApp for a quote on any route. Prices are per vehicle rather than per person, and the final amount is always confirmed on WhatsApp before your trip.

We recommend booking at least 24 to 48 hours ahead, especially for airport transfers and peak season travel, so we can guarantee your preferred vehicle. Last-minute and same-day requests are welcome too — our line is open 24/7 and we will tell you straight away what is available.

Absolutely. Choose "Multi-Day Tour" or "Custom Trip" on the booking form and tell us the places you want to see and how many days you have. We will put together a route, suggest stops along the way and send you a full quote.

We accept cash payment in Sri Lankan Rupees (LKR). If you would prefer a bank transfer or another payment method, message us on WhatsApp when you book and we will arrange it with you.
